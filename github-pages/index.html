UNIT 4 Tukey's Resistant Line vs Ordinary Least Squares (OLS)
Tukey's Resistant Line
y = a + bx
3-Group Robust Partition
OLS Linear Regression
y = c + mx
Least Squares Best Fit
Tukey 3-Group Medians (Left, Middle, Right)
-
Outer slope b = (y_R - y_L) / (x_R - x_L)
Theoretical Syllabus Proof:
Standard Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) minimizes squared vertical residuals ($\sum e_i^2$), making it vulnerable to leverage points and extreme ticket outliers. Tukey's Resistant Line partitions the ordered data into three equal batches (Left, Middle, Right) and derives the slope strictly from partition medians $(x_L, y_L)$ and $(x_R, y_R)$, providing robustness against heavy-tailed e-commerce purchases.

🎓 Academic Syllabus Mapping & Methodological Rigor
UNIT 4 SYLLABUS TOPICS

Bivariate Relationships & Robust Fitting

  • Two-Variable Relationships: Price vs Volume, Spend vs Quantity.
  • Percentage Tables: Row-wise conditional probabilities ($P(\text{Payment} \mid \text{Category})$) and column-wise distributions.
  • Contingency Tables & $\chi^2$: Cross-tabulation with Pearson's Chi-Square Test of Independence.
  • Handling Several Batches: Comparative boxplots across product departments.
  • Tukey's Resistant Line: Partitioning ordered data into 3 equal batches to compute median slope $b = \frac{y_R - y_L}{x_R - x_L}$.
UNIT 5 SYLLABUS TOPICS

Multivariate Analysis & Time Series

  • Third Conditioning Variable: Controlling for Customer Lifetime Spend Tiers.
  • Three-Variable Contingency: Category $\times$ Payment Method stratified by Spend Tier.
  • Correlation Matrix: Pearson pairwise correlation heatmap.
  • Time Series Fundamentals: Secular Trend, Seasonality, Cyclicality, and Noise decomposition.
  • Frequency Resampling: Converting timestamps to Daily (`D`), Weekly (`W`), Monthly (`M`), and Quarterly (`Q`) intervals.
